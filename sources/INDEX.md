## Known gaps
- Bhagavata Purana Skandhas 8-12 (incl. 10, Krishna's life): the scanned public-domain volumes found (Subba Rau vol. II 1928, Krishnacharya vol. II 1916) have unreadable OCR. Verify those Skandhas against a trusted online edition at research time and record the exact URL, Skandha, chapter and verse in RESEARCH.md.
